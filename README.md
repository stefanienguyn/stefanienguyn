# Hi, I'm Stefanie 👋

**Math & CS @ Pomona College · Sài Gòn, Vietnam → Claremont, CA · Vietnamese / English**

Born and raised in Sài Gòn, I'm a rising sophomore at Pomona College studying Math and Computer Science — which is honestly just a fancy way of saying I enjoy making things complicated before figuring out how to optimize them.

I jump around a little bit of pure math, a lot of wiring up robotics (and hoping they don't revolt), and a fair amount of computer vision and AI/ML to see how machines perceive the world.

When I'm not proving theorems or desperately debugging code, I'm probably out running, traveling, taking underexposed photos, or wandering around looking for my next adventure.
